 ## 问题

 1.$f(x)$是偶次非零多项式。 证明存在自然数$k$使得多项式$$f(x)+f(x+1)+\ldots+f(x+k)$$没有真正的根源。 
2.$f(x)$是奇次多项式。 证明存在自然数$k$使得多项式$$f(x)+f(x+1)+\ldots+f(x+k)$$仅有一个实根。 

S.贝尔洛夫，K.P.科哈斯

 圣彼得堡城市数学奥林匹克竞赛 (1995)

 ## 解决方案

 让$f(x)=a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_0$和$a_n\ne 0$，并定义为一个自然数$k$

$F_k(x)=\sum_{j=0}^k f(x+j),\qquad G_k(y)=\frac{F_k(ky)}{k^{n+1}}.$那么对于$0\le j\le k$和有界的$y$,$f(ky+j)=a_n(ky+j)^n+O(k^{n-1})$一致，因为较低阶项贡献$O(k^{n-1})$。 总结$j=0,\dots,k$给出$F_k(ky)=a_n\sum_{j=0}^k(ky+j)^n+O(k^n),$并除以$k^{n+1}$产量$G_k(y)=a_n\frac1k\sum_{j=0}^k\left(y+\frac jk\right)^n+O\!\left(\frac1k\right).$第一项是积分的黎曼和$(y+t)^n$超过$t\in[0,1]$，因此在紧凑间隔上均匀分布

 =\frac{a_n}{n+1}\bigl((y+1)^{n+1}-y^{n+1}\bigr)。$$### Part 1 Assume $n$ is even. Then $n+1$ is odd. If $H(y)=0$, then (y+1)^{n+1}=y^{n+1}. Since $t\mapsto t^{n+1}$ is strictly monotone on $\mathbb R$, this is impossible. Thus $H$ has no real roots. Let $R>0$ be arbitrary. Since $H$ is continuous and never vanishes, there exists $c>0$ such that $|H(y)|\ge c$ for all $y\in[-R,R]$. Uniform convergence of $G_k$ to $H$ on $[-R,R]$ guarantees that there exists $k_0$ such that for all $k\ge k_0$, |G_k(y)-H(y)|<\frac c2 for all $y\in[-R,R]$, which implies that $G_k$ has no roots in $[-R,R]$. To control roots for $|y|>R$, write G_k(y)=a_n\frac1k\sum_{j=0}^k\left(y+\frac jk\right)^n+P_k(y), where $P_k(y)$ is a polynomial of degree at most $n-1$. The coefficient of $y^n$ in the first term equals a_n\frac1k\sum_{j=0}^k1 =a_n\frac{k+1}{k},$$因此$$
G_k(y)=a_n\frac{k+1}{k}y^n+Q_k(y),
where $\deg Q_k\le n-1$. Fix $k$. Since $\deg Q_k\le n-1$, there is a constant $C_k>0$ such that
|Q_k(y)|\le C_k|y|^{,n-1}
\qquad (|y|\ge1).
$$选择$$
R_k=\max!\left(1,\frac{2C_k}{|a_n|(k+1)/k}\right).
Then for $|y|\ge R_k$,
|Q_k(y)|
\le C_k|y|^{n-1}
\le \frac{|a_n|(k+1)}{2k}|y|^n.
$$最后，$$
|G_k(y)|
\ge \frac{|a_n|(k+1)}{k}|y|^n-|Q_k(y)|
\ge \frac{|a_n|(k+1)}{2k}|y|^n>0.
$$而且，$$
# \operatorname{sgn} G_k(y)
\operatorname{sgn}!\left(a_n y^n\right)
\qquad (|y|\ge R_k).
Since $n$ is even, $a_n y^n$ has the same sign for all sufficiently large positive and negative $y$. Thus $G_k$ has no real roots with $|y|\ge R_k$. Now choose $R=R_k$. The preceding compact-interval argument shows that for all sufficiently large $k$, $G_k$ has no roots in $[-R_k,R_k]$, while the estimate above shows that there are no roots outside this interval. Hence, for sufficiently large $k$, $G_k$ has no real roots. Since $G_k(y)=0$ if and only if $F_k(ky)=0$, the polynomial $F_k$ also has no real roots. ### Part 2 Assume $n$ is odd. Then $n+1$ is even, and
H(y)=0
\iff
(y+1)^{n+1}=y^{n+1}
\iff
|y+1|=|y|.
$$唯一的解决方案是$$
y_0=-\frac12.
$$计算导数$$
H'(y)=a_n\bigl((y+1)^n-y^n\bigr),
$$和$$
# H'!\left(-\frac12\right)
# a_n\left(\left(\frac12\right)^n-\left(-\frac12\right)^n\right)
2a_n\left(\frac12\right)^n
\ne0,
so $y_0$ is a simple root. Choose $\varepsilon>0$ such that the interval
I=\left[y_0-\varepsilon,;y_0+\varepsilon\right]
contains no other roots of $H$. Since $H'$ is continuous and $H'(y_0)\neq0$, shrinking $\varepsilon$ if necessary, there exists $m>0$ such that
|H'(y)|\ge m
\qquad (y\in I).
Hence $H$ is strictly monotone on $I$. Let
\delta=\frac12\min\bigl(|H(y_0-\varepsilon)|,;|H(y_0+\varepsilon)|\bigr)>0.
Then $H(y_0-\varepsilon)$ and $H(y_0+\varepsilon)$ have opposite signs, and
|H(y_0\pm\varepsilon)|\ge2\delta.
Uniform convergence on $I$ yields a number $k_1$ such that for all $k\ge k_1$,
|G_k(y)-H(y)|<\delta
\qquad (y\in I).
For such $k$,
G_k(y_0-\varepsilon)
$$具有相同的符号$$
H(y_0-\varepsilon),
$$和$$
G_k(y_0+\varepsilon)
$$具有相同的符号$$
H(y_0+\varepsilon).
Since these signs are opposite, the intermediate value theorem gives at least one root of $G_k$ in $I$. To prove uniqueness, suppose $u,v\in I$ are roots of $G_k$. Then
H(u)=H(u)-G_k(u),
\qquad
H(v)=H(v)-G_k(v),
$$所以$$
|H(u)|<\delta,\qquad |H(v)|<\delta.
By the mean value theorem and the bound $|H'|\ge m$ on $I$,
|H(u)-H(v)|\ge m|u-v|.
$$还，$$
|H(u)-H(v)|
\le |H(u)|+|H(v)|
<2\delta.
$$因此$$
 |u-v|<\frac{2\delta}{m}。 
选择$\delta$如果必要的话更小，这会迫使任意两个根$I$一致。 因此$G_k$恰好有一个根$I$。 在$\mathbb R\setminus I$，连续函数$H$没有零。 因此存在$\delta_1>0$这样
 |H(y)|\ge2\delta_1
 \qquad（是\而不是我）。 
在包含所有可能的实根的每个紧区间上均匀收敛$G_k$然后给出
 |G_k(y)-H(y)|<\delta_1,
 所以$G_k(y)\neq0$在$\mathbb R\setminus I$。 因此外部没有额外的根$I$。 因此对于足够大的$k$,$G_k$仅有一个实根。 重新缩放$x=ky$表明$F_k$也只有一个实根。 两种说法均成立。 ∎
